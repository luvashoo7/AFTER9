Create a complete, production-quality, responsive website for a Gen-Z focused night-commerce startup called:

AFTER 9

Brand styling:
- Write the brand as “AFTER 9”
- The “9” should be visually distinctive and can be treated almost like a brand symbol.
- Tagline direction: “Whatever you need after 9.”
- Secondary positioning: “Night commerce. Delivered instantly.”
- Core idea: If you need something at night, AFTER 9 is the app that comes to mind first.

BUSINESS CONCEPT

AFTER 9 is an instant night-commerce delivery platform initially launching as a pilot in Greater Noida, India.

Operating window:
9 PM – 5/6 AM

Initial service area:
Greater Noida

Future expansion:
Noida → Delhi NCR → major Indian cities

The platform delivers everyday packaged products during the night, especially products people suddenly need after regular stores and many delivery options become less convenient.

Product categories can include:
- Packaged snacks
- Chips
- Chocolates
- Cold drinks / soft drinks
- Eggs
- Instant food
- Biscuits
- Personal-care essentials
- Household essentials
- Small packaged grocery items
- ₹10 / low-price sachets and small packs
- Party packs
- Midnight craving bundles
- Emergency essentials
- Other permitted packaged products

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT visually or verbally make the website primarily about cigarettes, alcohol, tobacco, or other restricted products.
The brand should feel like a broad night-essentials platform, not a tobacco delivery website.
Use legal/compliant product categories and clearly communicate that availability depends on local laws and regulations.

CORE USP

The biggest differentiator is:

OPEN-BOX DELIVERY + INSTANT RETURN

The customer does NOT have to blindly accept the package.

When the delivery partner arrives:
1. Customer receives the package.
2. Customer can open and visually inspect it at the doorstep.
3. If an item is wrong, damaged, missing, or doesn't match the order, the customer can immediately request a return/rejection through the app.
4. The delivery partner processes the return instantly.
5. No waiting for a traditional return pickup.

Position this as:

“See it. Check it. Accept it.”

or

“Open before you accept.”

The experience should communicate TRUST and CONTROL rather than just speed.
